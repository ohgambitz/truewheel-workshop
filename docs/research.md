# Stage 1: Research and planning

Date: 2026-11-27. Brand: Truewheel Workshop, a sample local wheel-building and bicycle-service business. Name, prices, hours and copy are illustrative until the owner supplies real details.

## Audience
- Local road, gravel and commuter riders who need servicing and want to know the price and turnaround before they visit.
- Enthusiasts considering a hand-built wheelset, comparing parts, weight and cost.
- Mostly on phones (they find the shop by search or social), so the mobile layout is the primary design target.

## What customers look for (evidence)
- Online booking and upfront prices rank highest. A bike-shop marketing source reports 67% prefer booking online and 34% of online bookings happen outside opening hours ([Hubtiger](https://hubtiger.com/bike-repair-store-success-formula/)). An auto-service survey found over 70% rate online service and price information as very or extremely important ([Openbay via Aftermarket News](https://www.aftermarketnews.com/openbay-launches-new-online-service-help-automotive-care-businesses-better-serve-consumers-immediate-need-auto-repair-service/)). These are directional: no bike-specific survey with a published method was found.
- Turnaround matters: long waits push riders to other shops ([ZipDo, aggregator, unverified](https://zipdo.co/customer-experience-in-the-bicycle-industry-statistics/)).

## Competitor scan (patterns, not designs to copy)
| Business | Pattern worth noting |
|---|---|
| [Fanatik Bike](https://www.fanatikbike.com/wheel-builder) | Online wheel builder: pick rim, hub, spokes |
| [Dubya USA](https://www.cyclenews.com/2022/03/article/dubya-usa-custom-crafted-wheels/) | Configure a wheel, get a no-obligation quote |
| [Blue Lug](https://bluelug.com/en/contents/wheel) | States a 2-week lead time; price = parts + build fee |
| [Cosmic Bikes](https://dodecahedron-wolverine-b6ba.squarespace.com/specialty-bicycle-wheels) | States a 1 to 2 week turnaround |
| [Condor Cycles](https://condorcycles.com/pages/wheel-building) | Workshop booking; customers can supply their own parts |

## Implications for the site
1. Lead with booking and price clarity; keep "Book a service" one tap away on every screen size.
2. Keep the live wheelset configurator (competitive norm) and show the price as parts plus build labour.
3. State turnaround per service, as competitors do.
4. Differentiate on craft: show the tension-chart idea and real imagery of a laced wheel, not stock photos.
5. Do not present booking as working until it is connected to a real inbox or booking system.
