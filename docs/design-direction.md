# Stage 2: Design direction

Keep and harden the current identity, which already passed a design critique and suits the subject:
- **Steel-frame green and brass**: classic bicycle paint and brass fittings. One accent only (brass).
- **Condensed display type** (Big Shoulders Display) like frame decals, with Instrument Sans for reading.
- **Signature element**: a live spoke-tension chart that trues itself, the thing a wheel builder actually reads. Kept as the hero.
- **Real imagery**: rendered images of a laced 32-spoke wheel (assembled and exploded) made from a 3D model of the product category. No stock photos, no people, no invented workshop photos.
- **Conversion first**: sticky booking action, prices on the wall, configurator, short booking form.
- Removed: the placeholder reviews section (placeholders read as unfinished); it returns when real reviews exist.
